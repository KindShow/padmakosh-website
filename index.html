THE MEANING OF PADMAKOSH

“Padmakosh is inspired by a hastamudra that depicts a blooming lotus in Indian classical tradition — the timeless symbol of aesthetics, wealth, wisdom, and enlightenment.”

Padmakosh Studio in Bavdhan, Pune is home to Padmakosh School of Kathak — serving nearby areas including Kothrud, Warje, Pashan, Aundh, and Baner, as well as global learners online.

EMOTIONAL STORYTELLING

Abhinaya & Poetic Dialogue

Under Sonal Pendse’s guidance, abhinaya delves into the subtle nuances of bhava and rasa. Students learn to convey devotion, longing, and philosophical inquiry through glance, breath, and micro-expressions.

MATHEMATICAL RIGOUR

Taal, Padhant & Certified Exams

Padhant — the vocal recitation of bols — is the heartbeat of Kathak. Students internalize Tritaal, Jhaptal, and Pancham Sawari while preparing for twice-yearly examinations with Gandharva Mahavidyalaya and Bharati Vidyapeeth.

  • — Toda, tukda, paran, and chakkar execution with rhythmic stability
  • — Certification examinations conducted twice a year for global recognition

Where is Padmakosh School of Kathak & Padmakosh Studio located in Pune?

Padmakosh Studio is located at Lohia Jain Square in Bavdhan, Pune (411021), with direct connectivity via NDA-Pashan Road and the Mumbai-Bangalore Highway — making it a convenient destination for students seeking Kathak classes and a professional dance studio near Bavdhan, Kothrud, Pashan, Aundh, Baner, and Warje.

Which examination boards is Padmakosh School of Kathak affiliated with?

We are affiliated with Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya and Bharati Vidyapeeth for globally recognized Kathak certification examinations, which are conducted twice a year.

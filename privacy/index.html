DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Padmakosh School of Kathak and Padmakosh Studio respect your privacy and handle student and enquiry data with utmost care.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an admission or studio space enquiry, we collect only your name, contact information (email or phone number), chosen category, and message. We do not solicit or store sensitive personal information or financial documents through this website.

2. Use of Information

Submitted details are used solely to reply to your question, assess class level placement, or coordinate studio bookings in Bavdhan, Pune. We never sell or distribute your contact details to third-party marketing entities.

3. Embedded Media

Video players across this site use privacy-conscious click-to-load embeds on youtube-nocookie.com, ensuring third-party cookies are not placed until you choose to interact with a video.

4. Contact

For any questions regarding privacy or to update your contact information, please email sonalpatil286@gmail.com.

5. Institutional & Event Faculty Disclosure

Padmakosh School of Kathak and Padmakosh Studio are directed by Sonal Pendse in Bavdhan, Pune. For music-related events, classical baithaks, and music workshops hosted at Padmakosh Studio, Shri. Anil Pendse and Smt. Anjali Pendse are associated with the studio as music instructors.

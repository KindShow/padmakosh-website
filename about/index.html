The Blooming Lotus of
Classical Kathak.

Padmakosh — the name is inspired by a hastamudra (hand gesture) that shows a blooming lotus in Indian classical tradition. A lotus is the timeless symbol of aesthetics, wealth, wisdom, and enlightenment.

Guided by Kathak artist Sonal Pendse in Bavdhan, Pune, Padmakosh Studio is home to Padmakosh School of Kathak — affiliated with Gandharva Mahavidyalaya and Bharati Vidyapeeth for globally recognized certification examinations.

VISION & ETHOS

Our Motto, Virtues & Modern Learning

Bridging time-honoured guru-shishya discipline with contemporary teaching infrastructure for learners of every age group.

01 · OUR MOTTO

Personalized Modules & Certified Boards

Padmakosh Studio is home to Padmakosh School of Kathak. We offer personalized modules of Kathak training for all age groups through weekday and weekend batches of certified examination boards having worldwide recognition.

02 · OUR VIRTUES

Discipline, Technique & Versatility

Personal attention, disciplined teaching, and a versatile approach shape a dancer with strong technique and open the doors to immense artistic and professional possibilities.

03 · PHYSICAL & VIRTUAL CLASSES

Hybrid & Virtual Learning

Along with in-person physical classes at our Bavdhan studio, we conduct interactive virtual classes supported by high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity and dedicated audiovisual facilities for detailed footwork and mudra observation.

Associated Music Instructors for Studio Events: Music-related events, classical baithaks, and accompaniment workshops hosted at Padmakosh Studio are conducted in association with music instructors Shri. Anil Pendse and Smt. Anjali Pendse.

ACCREDITED EXAMINATION AFFILIATIONS

Affiliated to Gandharva Mahavidyalaya & Bharati Vidyapeeth

We are officially affiliated with Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Mandal and Bharati Vidyapeeth for recognized certification examinations in the field of Kathak — empowering students across Pune and worldwide with accredited credentials.

FROM STUDENT TO SOLO PERFORMER

Our Ongoing Quest for Perfection

Every dancer dreams to be a solo performer — so prestigious competitions, annual showcase performances, and choreography productions are introduced throughout the learning process. Our quest for perfection is made possible through six foundational pillars:

WORLDWIDE RECOGNITION 01

Certification Exams Twice a Year

Structured academic progression affiliated with Gandharva Mahavidyalaya and Bharati Vidyapeeth, with formal certification examinations conducted twice every year.

STAGE CONFIDENCE 02

Annual Showcase Performances

Dedicated performing opportunities in our flagship annual shows, allowing dancers across every batch and age group to experience the joy of theatrical stage presentation.

ARTISTIC INDIVIDUALITY 03

Solo Presentation & Competitions

Every dancer dreams to be a solo performer. We introduce prestigious competitions, solo repertoire mentorship, and choreography productions throughout the learning journey.

PROFESSIONAL STAGE 04

Commercial & Cultural Shows

Regular performance opportunities in curated commercial and cultural productions after attaining technical mastery and stage maturity.

PEDAGOGICAL LINEAGE 05

Teacher’s Training & Experience

Guided teacher-training modules and hands-on classroom mentoring opportunities for senior disciples aspiring to pass on classical parampara.

ARTS LEADERSHIP 06

Studio Management Opportunities

Practical working opportunities in studio operations, rehearsal coordination, and arts management at Padmakosh Studio in Bavdhan, Pune.

This document records the original website copy written for Padmakosh School of Kathak and Padmakosh Studio, strictly separating **Verified Source Facts**, **Owner-Supplied Draft Copy (for sign-off)**, and **Explicit Placeholders**.

## 1. Editorial Rules Enforced
1. **No Copied Legacy Boilerplate:** All copy is freshly written; generic Kathak history and therapeutic claims from the legacy site were not copied.
2. **No Unsupported Superlatives:** Terms such as *"leading"* or *"best"* and unverified review counters (*4.7/5 from 37 reviews*) are omitted from evergreen copy and structured data.
3. **Clear Institution Hierarchy:** **Padmakosh School of Kathak** is the primary classical institution; **Padmakosh Studio** is presented distinctly as the physical Bavdhan space and secondary rental/workshop offering.
4. **Honest Empty States:** When no upcoming events are verified, the site displays: `"Upcoming events will be announced here."`
5. **Conservative CTA:** Default call-to-action is `"Enquire about Kathak classes"`, with a separate `"Enquire about Padmakosh Studio"` category and zero promise of a free trial or instant automated response.
